What FundMap does

FundMap researches which grants your organization can actually apply for, and delivers a short brief: the funder, the program, the amount range, the deadline, and why it fits you. We screen out foundations that do not accept unsolicited applications, and every line is checked against the funder's own live website before delivery and stamped with the date it was verified — because foundation data in bulk databases is one to two years old.

Data sources: IRS Form 990-PF filings, Grants.gov, and each funder's own website (the funder's site is the final authority for every deadline we print). We also use free public lookup tools to cross-check.

Currently Texas nonprofits only; first focus is youth services and education. Best fit: 501(c)(3) organizations with an annual budget under $500,000.

What "verified" means

Every match in a FundMap brief is verified on three points before delivery:

  • Accepts applications — the funder accepts unsolicited applications (we screen out foundations that only fund pre-selected organizations).
  • Deadline verified — the deadline is checked on the funder's own live website, not in a bulk database, and stamped with the date it was checked.
  • Fit verified — the amount range, geography (Texas), and eligibility criteria match your organization's profile.
