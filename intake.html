FundMap brief — intake

Thank you for your order. To build your grant-matching brief, email your answers to the questions below to studytrack.shop@gmail.com with the subject line "FundMap intake — [your organization name]".

  1. Organization legal name
  2. Website (if any)
  3. City / county in Texas
  4. Mission in 1–2 sentences — who you serve and how
  5. Annual budget range (e.g. under $100k / $100–250k / $250–500k)
  6. What you want funded, and roughly how much (e.g. "$40–90k for tutor stipends and a summer program")
  7. Any deadlines or grant cycles you already know about (optional)
  8. Any funders you have applied to in the last 2 years (optional — helps us avoid duplicates)

Your brief is delivered by email within 5 business days of receiving your intake answers. Every match is checked against the funder's own live website before delivery and date-stamped.

FundMap is an information service only — no application writing, no submission, no guarantee of funding.
